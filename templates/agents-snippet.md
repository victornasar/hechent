## Hechent

- Before the first edit for any request to change code, config, or docs, use the `assess` skill.
- When decisions must be settled with the user before building, or a decision already agreed is unsettled during implementation, use the `grill` skill.
- When asked to verify or self-review a change, and before saying a change works or opening a PR, use the `verify` skill. An obvious, narrowly scoped Light change may be checked directly, as `assess` describes.
